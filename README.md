# Ira Grow — Award-Winning Digital Flagship Redesign

> **"FROM FIELDS TO FRONTIERS."**  
> A luxury, animation-rich digital transformation of [iragrow.com](https://iragrow.com/) inspired by Awwwards & FWA Site of the Day design standards.

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Design Innovations

1. **Aesthetic Direction**:
   - **Earthy Luxury Noir Palette**: Deep obsidian (`#060907`), imperial gold (`#d4af37`), turmeric amber (`#e59a3c`), and fresh agricultural emerald (`#2e7d4e`).
   - **Editorial Typography**: Pairing classical display serifs (*Playfair Display*, *Cinzel*) with high-precision modern sans-serifs (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Space Grotesk*).
   - **Tactile Depth**: 35mm film grain overlay texture, blurred frosted glassmorphism, and ambient dynamic light auras.

2. **Animations & Micro-Interactions**:
   - **Dynamic Golden Grain Canvas**: Interactive HTML5 particle engine simulating floating turmeric spores and golden harvest grains with mouse magnetic repulsion.
   - **GSAP & ScrollTrigger Choreography**: Split reveals, staggered cards, and dynamic numeric counters for statistics.
   - **3D Card Perspective Tilt**: Interactive physics calculating cursor position on product cards and accreditation badges.
   - **Custom Magnetic Cursor**: Dual-ring smooth-lerping cursor follower with expansion on interactive elements.
   - **Web Audio API Ambient Engine**: Synthesizes warm harmonic frequencies and gentle metallic chime micro-feedback without external audio assets.

3. **Interactive Business Features**:
   - **Interactive 3D Product Matrix**: Inspect Turmeric (Curcumin 3–5%), Cumin Seeds (Singapore & European bold quality), and Yellow Maize with technical drawers (purity, moisture, packing).
   - **Interactive Global Maritime Corridors**: Dynamic map explorer detailing transit times and cargo demand to Dubai (Jebel Ali), Europe (Rotterdam), Southeast Asia (Singapore), and Africa (Mombasa/Durban).
   - **Smart Export RFQ & Container Calculator**: Allows international procurement teams to select commodity, IPM vs. Conventional grade, 25kg/50kg PP bags or Jumbo loads, and volume in Metric Tons. Instantly outputs container load equivalents (20' vs 40' FCL) and formats 1-click inquiries to WhatsApp (+91 78881 89622) and official export email (`exports@iragrow.com`).
   - **Quality & Certifications Vault**: APEDA, Spices Board of India, FSSAI, and Phytosanitary documentation showcase.
